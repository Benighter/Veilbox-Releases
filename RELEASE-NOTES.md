# Veilbox 1.0.0

First release for Windows and Android. Create disposable addresses and manage their incoming mail together or individually in a locally encrypted vault.

Includes address categories, queued batch creation, combined and individual inboxes, verification-code copying, starred mail, archive and local Bin, sender filters, mute controls, signup tracking, secure unsubscribe links, attachment saving, encrypted backup export and restore, and an Android update checker.

Mailboxes are temporary and receive-only. Provider limits and site acceptance apply. Signup records do not create or cancel subscriptions. Devices share data through encrypted backups, not automatic synchronization.

Windows live email delivery and vault controls were verified. The Android package was compiled and its signing certificate verified. Physical-phone installation and update testing remain separate checks.
